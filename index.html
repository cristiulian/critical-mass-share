LFO offsets

Drag a knob up or down to change the sound (the LFO offsets: where each shape's cycle starts); double-tap one to put it back. Copying, downloading and sending use the sound as you set it.

Tap a key. Each note is played by the FM-1's own synth, here in your browser.

Put it on your FM-1

Over USB, in Chrome or Edge on a computer, or Chrome on Android (a USB-C cable). iPhones and iPads can't reach the FM-1 from a page: copy the code instead and use the Librarian on a computer.

The code (CM1:…) goes into the Critical Mass Librarian, a chat or a forum post; the file (.cmpatch.json) into the Librarian or the command line tools.

Sounds shared from an FM-1

On a Critical Mass FM-1, hold SAVE for half a second: the sound appears as a QR code. Scan it with a phone's camera and it opens here, to hear and to keep. The sound travels inside the link itself: nothing is uploaded or stored anywhere.

The sound lives in this page's link: nothing was sent to any server.
